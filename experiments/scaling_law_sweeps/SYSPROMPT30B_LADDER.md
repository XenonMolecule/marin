# System-prompt pretraining ladder (DCLM-30B corpus)

Five model sizes, four data conditions each, 20 models. Every model is a Qwen3-architecture
decoder (Llama-3 rotary embeddings) trained from scratch with the Llama-3.1 tokenizer
(vocab 128,256) at sequence length 4096 for exactly one epoch of its own data.

## The four conditions (arms)

| arm | name | what the model saw |
|---|---|---|
| A | sysprompt | every document with a generated system prompt prepended: `<\|start_header_id\|>system<\|end_header_id\|>\n\n{S}<\|eot_id\|>{D}` |
| B | token-matched | A's documents plus extra unlabeled documents, no prompts, same total tokens as A |
| C | doc-matched | exactly A's documents, no prompts (~1.7% fewer tokens than A) |
| D | mix50 | exactly A's documents; a content-hashed half get the prompt, the other half don't |

Prompts are one-line descriptions (~16 tokens, ~21 with the wrapper) generated by
Qwen3-30B-A3B. Smaller rungs use an exact prefix of the 998M document set, and D's
half is keyed on `doc_id`, so a document conditioned at one scale is conditioned at all.

## Scale, compute, and tokens

Compute budget is the delphi isoFLOP grid cell; batch, learning rate and other
hyperparameters are the frozen values for that cell and are identical across arms.

| rung | params | width / layers | FLOPs | batch (seqs) | tokens A | tokens B | tokens C | tokens D | tok/param |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 210M | 210M | 640 / 7 | 3e18 | 16 | 3.027B | 3.030B | 2.979B | 3.003B | 14 |
| 358M | 358M | 896 / 10 | 9e18 | 32 | 4.707B | 4.709B | 4.631B | 4.669B | 13 |
| 447M | 447M | 1024 / 11 | 1.8e19 | 32 | 7.280B | 7.283B | 7.160B | 7.220B | 16 |
| 669M | 669M | 1280 / 13 | 3e19 | 32 | 7.722B | 7.724B | 7.595B | 7.659B | 12 |
| 998M | 998M | 1536 / 16 | 9e19 | 64 | 14.810B | 14.815B | 14.566B | 14.688B | 15 |

Tokens are the tokenized-cache totals (`train/.stats.json`), including BOS/EOS. Steps
are `tokens / (batch x 4096)`, so C runs ~1.7% fewer steps than A and B, and D ~0.8% fewer.
C is therefore ~1.7% below the nominal FLOP budget by design (no document is ever repeated).
