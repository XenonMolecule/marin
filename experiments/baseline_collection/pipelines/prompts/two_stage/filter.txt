THE READER TEST: imagine someone who came to this page on purpose. Would they READ it — start at the top of its text and follow it to the end — or would they LOOK IT OVER and click somewhere else?

Pages people read: articles, stories, explanations, tutorials, arguments, answers with their questions, discussions worth following, reference entries that explain, code — a source file is read by a programmer the way an article is read by anyone else — and study material: flashcards, vocabulary sets and revision notes are studied line by line, which is reading.
